# AI search and answer readiness
1. Use actual Search Console question queries and verified service/location facts; do not fabricate AI visibility metrics.
2. Check crawlable, clear answers to buyer questions and distinct source URLs, structured headings and natural internal links.
3. Review JSON-LD only if it describes real verifiable business information. Avoid claims of endorsements or guaranteed AI recommendation.
4. For fees, grants, legal rules, energy claims, guarantees and years, verify an authoritative current source before publication.
5. Favor factual explanations and real case studies over mass-generated FAQ pages or misleading schema.
6. A new FAQ route, new public page or sitewide schema rewrite requires owner approval.
Report target questions, evidence, affected URLs, recommended improvement and how to monitor.
